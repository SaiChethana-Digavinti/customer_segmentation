## 1. Executive Summary

This report provides an in-depth evaluation of customer purchasing patterns, behavioral clustering, and targeted marketing strategies generated by the **Customer Segmentation Platform**.

By combining **RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** with **Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means Clustering)** and **Generative AI Marketing Recommendations**, customers are partitioned into clear behavioral tiers. This segmentation empowers the business to:
- Protect high-value revenue streams by rewarding VIP customers.
- Proactively salvage at-risk accounts before total churn occurs.
- Accelerate repeat transactions and average order values (AOV) across loyal and new customer cohorts.

## 3. Machine Learning Clustering Evaluation

The customer segmentation model uses standard Euclidean distance over standardized RFM attributes:

$$\text{Features} = [\text{Recency}_{\text{scaled}}, \text{Frequency}_{\text{scaled}}, \text{Monetary}_{\text{scaled}}]$$

### Model Selection & Elbow Analysis
Evaluation of $K \in [2, 10]$ identified $K = 4$ as the optimal balance between within-cluster sum of squares (Inertia) and cluster cohesion:

| Clusters ($K$) | Inertia (WCSS) | Silhouette Score | Evaluation Notes |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 2 | 6483.59 | 0.4328 | Oversimplified (merges Champions & Loyal) |
| 3 | 4869.49 | 0.3365 | Suboptimal boundary separation |
| **4 (Selected)** | **3939.05** | **0.3375** | **Optimal distinct business personas** |
| 5 | 3296.71 | 0.3162 | Diminishing returns; fragmenting clusters |
| 6 | 2855.76 | 0.3124 | Over-segmentation for actionable campaigns |

## 4. Segment Deep-Dives & AI Marketing Playbook

### 4.1 Champions (VIP Cohort)
- **Profile**: Recent activity (avg. 20.7 days), highest monetary value (avg. ₹920.00, up to ₹1,600.00), high RFM total (9 to 11).
- **Revenue Contribution**: **80.1% of total revenue**.
- **Customer IDs**: 1007, 1013, 1010, 1003, 1005, 1015, 1008.
- **Campaign**: *VIP Rewards Campaign*
- **Action Plan**:
  1. Provide dedicated concierge customer support and early access to product releases.
  2. Deliver tier-based VIP status rewards and bespoke appreciation gifts.
  3. Solicit product reviews and invite to beta testing programs.

### 4.2 Loyal Customers
- **Profile**: Moderate recency (avg. 41.3 days), consistent repeat buyers, modest order sizes (avg. ₹141.25).
- **Revenue Contribution**: **7.0% of total revenue**.
- **Customer IDs**: 1012, 1014, 1009, 1006.
- **Campaign**: *Loyalty Program Campaign*
- **Action Plan**:
  1. Deploy personalized cross-selling and bundling recommendations based on purchase history.
  2. Introduce points-based loyalty milestones to incentivize higher basket sizes.
  3. Offer free shipping thresholds to elevate average order value (AOV).

### 4.3 At Risk
- **Profile**: Lapsed purchase frequency (avg. 74.5 days since last order), average spend ₹255.00.
- **Revenue Contribution**: **6.3% of total revenue**.
- **Customer IDs**: 1011, 1002.
- **Campaign**: *Win-Back Campaign*
- **Action Plan**:
  1. Trigger automated re-engagement email sequence with time-limited discounts (e.g., 20% off next order).
  2. Send "We Miss You" surveys to diagnose dissatisfaction points or service gaps.
  3. Retarget via social channels with popular bestselling products.

### 4.4 Lost Customers
- **Profile**: High dormancy (avg. 100+ days inactive), baseline historical spend ₹260.00.
- **Revenue Contribution**: **6.5% of total revenue**.
- **Customer IDs**: 1001, 1004.
- **Campaign**: *Reactivation Campaign*
- **Action Plan**:
  1. Low-cost automated email reactivation blast highlighting major product catalog updates.
  2. Offer deep discount clearance or aggressive renewal incentive.
  3. Suppress costly physical outreach to protect marketing ROI.

## 6. Live API Export Endpoints

The system exposes programmatic REST endpoints for real-time report generation and automated export:

- `GET /customers/export/csv` — Filterable customer CSV export (`?segment=Champions&priority=High&search=...`).
- `GET /customers/export/segments-summary/csv` — Segment summary CSV export.
- `GET /customers/{customer_id}/ai-recommendation` — Dynamic Gemini AI marketing copy.
- `POST /dataset/segment` — Upload any custom CSV/Excel/JSON file to recalculate RFM, clustering, and segmentation dynamically.
